How to use

  1. Tap the event name at the top and choose the event.
  2. Choose Time in when students arrive, or Time out when they leave.
  3. Scan the QR code on each student's card. Green is a good scan, amber means already scanned, red means the ID is unknown.
  4. Forgot a card? Type the ID under the camera.
  5. No signal? Keep scanning. Scans are kept on the phone and sent when you are back online.
